**To the Night Sky**

The setting sun whispered farewell to the day,  
Amidst the mountains, a serene lake lay.  
Down from the hills tumble cascading apples,  
Diving into the water with splashes and ripples.

In the night air, her azure hair swayed with grace,  
As a gentle zephyr caressed the girl's face.  
Looking up to the starry, starry sky,  
Where hides the glowing, broken crescent moon.

Grasping a glass shard, reaching for the high,  
Are those twinkling stars just fragments of the moon?  
Translucent as it was,  
Luminous as they were.
