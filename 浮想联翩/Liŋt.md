**Liŋt**
XiangSora196 2026.04.12

The rain visited the town as usual.

Alone in the room I sat beside the window. 

Beneath the grey sky fell a gentle drizzle. 

On the pebbled lane it formed a ripple. 

Slipping over the roof, dripping from the eaves,

A raindrop vanished without a trace in my outstretched hand.

No one put up their umbrellas, nobody urges to leave,  

Just strolling around in their sun, blind to my ever-raining land.

My sorrow shall be sung through the notes of the rain.

Let the wordless chorus wash away my unspoken pain. 

Mock me not, even if the world before was all an illusion. 

Drowning myself in the rain of lament is my only redemption.
