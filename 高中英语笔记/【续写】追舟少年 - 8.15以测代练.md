```
When we arrived in Tournus, my cousin Gabriel and I parked our bikes near the dock (码头) and walked to the town square. We'd just ridden 33 kilometers. We were starving and planned to get lunch and then ride back to Macon, a small city in France on the Saône River. I was spending the summer there with Gabriel's family.

It was market day. Locals moved about, filling their baskets with groceries. Tourists hunted for souvenirs. Gabriel and I bought a long, thin loaf of bread called a baguette and some Brie, a soft cheese I'm obsessed with.

As we ate, I noticed a girl wearing a Basketball T - shirt. She smiled at us and stopped to buy pastries with someone that I guessed was her mom because they looked so much alike.

I saw them again by the river. They sat on a bench enjoying their pastries in front of a long, narrow riverboat named Voyageur, which had docked (靠岸) earlier. Then they boarded the boat. It was preparing to leave when I spotted a colorful shopping bag on the bench. The girl had left it! I looked up. Some passengers were gathering on the upper deck of the riverboat, including the girl and her mom.

I held up the bag and waved it to get their attention while Gabriel called to them in French and English. “Excusez - moi, mesdames! Excuse me, ladies! ”

When the girl noticed the bag, she opened her mouth in surprise. But her face fell as the boat started to pull away from the dock. She didn't think she'd get her stuff back.

‘Maybe we can throw it to her, Hugo! ” Gabriel said.

“It's too light, ”I said. “It would end up in the river. We have to find a way to return it! ”

Running along side the boat, Gabriel learned from a sailor that the boat's next stop was Macon — our destination as well. It would take the boat just under two hours.

“Do you think we can keep up? ”I asked.

Gabriel laughed. “We're about to find out! ”
```

这样写的逻辑是：

- **第一段** → 动作描写 + 心理描写 + 氛围（紧张感）。
- **第二段** → 情节收尾 + 情感升华（善意 +青春的意义）。

## 🌟 情节方面

优点：

- 有紧张感，追逐场景写得很有画面（“sweat blurring our sights”“cicadas chorusing monotonely”）
- 情绪张弛有度：前半段艰辛、煎熬 → 后半段温馨、甜美 → 有完整弧线。
- 结尾点题很好，“my days grew warm, whole and bright”带出升华。

可改进：

- 前半段骑行描写稍显“堆叠”，句子长、修辞多，但节奏可能有点拖慢紧迫感。可以适当缩短句子，让读者感到更“急切”。
- “10 kilos left”如果是口语，建议改成“10 kilometers left”更自然（否则像重量）。

---

## 🌟 语言方面

优点：

- 词汇很丰富（ordeal, monotonely, abyss, miraculously dispersed）。
- 有不少地道的修辞（“my heart skipped a beat” “sweetness immediately spreading over my tongue”）。
- 对比手法好（艰难 vs. 温暖）。

可改进：

1. **小语法/用词问题**
    - “Surrounded in the broiling air” → 建议改成 **“Surrounded by the broiling air”**。
    - “monotonely” → 正确副词是 **“monotonously”**。
    - “Macon'dock” → 应为 **“Macon’s dock”**。
    - “gratitude and unbelieving” → “unbelieving”这里不太搭，建议换成 **“gratitude and disbelief”**。
2. **表达更地道**
    - “the gears creaking in response” → 可以换成 **“the gears groaning in protest”** 更生动。
    - “rescued me from the abyss again and again” → 稍显夸张，可以改成 **“pulled me back from the brink of giving up”**。
    - “catalyzed by the girl's sweet smile” → “catalyzed”太学术感，可以用 **“brightened further by the girl’s sweet smile”**。
3. **句式优化**
    - “We urgently searched for that familiar figure, when a waving hand captured our attention.”  
        → 可以改成 **“We scanned the crowd anxiously until a waving hand suddenly caught our eyes.”**，更紧凑。

## 1️⃣ 关于 **as long as** 那句

你写的：

> "We cannot make it as long as we don't ride at full speed!"

问题：
- **as long as** 一般用在肯定条件（= if，只要……），放在否定里就不自然。
- 老师说得对，这里应该直接用 **if**。
👉 改法：
- “We won’t make it if we don’t ride at full speed!” ✅

---

## 2️⃣ 关于 **肩上重量 & 朋友鼓励** 的写法

你原来是合在一起的：

> The heaviness on my shoulder and the encouragement from my teammate rescued me from the abyss again and again.

问题：
- 两个意象放一起稍微拥挤。
- 可以把“肩上重负”放进**环境描写**（阳光、热、汗）里，让气氛更沉重。
- **朋友鼓励**单独拿出来，用强调句写出来，更突出作用。
👉 改法示例：
- _The burning sun, the endless road, and the bag weighing heavily on my shoulder almost crushed my will._
- _It was Gabriel’s voice—his shouts and laughter—that kept me going again and again._

---

## 3️⃣ 关于 **waving hand**

你写的：
> when a waving hand captured our attention.

问题：
- 这个动作没问题，但不够“呼应前文”。
- 既然前文特意写了 **Basketball T-shirt**，就可以在这里再次点出来，呼应，更有“线索感”。

👉 改法：
- _Just then, a flash of that Basketball T-shirt in the crowd caught our eyes._
- _Among the passengers, the familiar shirt stood out—she was waving at us._

这样更有画面感。

---

## 4️⃣ 关于 **倒数两句句式乱**

你写的：

> I gave a bite at the pastry, sweetness immediately spreading over my tongue, catalyzed by the girl's sweet smile. All the tiredness, exhaustion miraculously dispersed, and my days grew warm, whole and bright.

问题：
- 第一部分有点“头重脚轻”，分词结构太长。
- 第二部分 “tiredness, exhaustion miraculously dispersed” 有点重复，主谓宾关系也乱。

👉 改法（分成两句）：
- _I took a bite of the pastry, and sweetness spread across my tongue, brightened further by the girl’s smile._
- _In that moment, all the exhaustion melted away, leaving the summer warm, whole, and unforgettable._

---

## 5️⃣ 关于你提的小词汇问题

- **push the pedal** → 正常说法是 **“press the pedals”** 或 **“pedal harder”**。
    - e.g. _I pressed the pedals harder, the bike jolting forward._
- **squeeze** （挤过去）
    - 你写 _squeezing ourselves across the crowd_ 稍微不自然。
    - 常见搭配：**squeeze through the crowd / squeeze our way through the crowd**。
    - e.g. _We squeezed our way through the crowd, breathless but determined._

## 🏁 场景一：出发（They start chasing on bikes）

**写作重点**

- 紧迫感：动作迅速，没时间犹豫。
- 环境描写：阳光、热气、河边风。
- 动作描写：骑车的姿态、声音。

**语言素材**

- 动作：
    - _We leapt onto our bikes and set off at full speed._
    - _I pressed the pedals harder, the gears groaning in protest._
    - _The wheels spun furiously as we darted forward._
- 环境：
    - _The scorching sun beat down on our backs._
    - _Hot wind whipped past my face as we sped along the river._
    - _The cicadas droned endlessly, echoing our pounding hearts._
- 心理：
    - _My pulse quickened, every second a reminder that the boat was drifting farther away._
    - _There was no turning back—we had to catch it._

## 🚴 场景二：艰苦骑行（The long ride）

**写作重点**
- 描写“困境”：炎热、体力透支、汗水模糊视线。
- 展现“坚持”：咬牙忍耐、互相鼓励。
- 可以对比“几乎放弃 vs. 再度振作”。

**语言素材**
- 环境/身体：
    - _Sweat stung my eyes and blurred my sight._
    - _The bag weighed heavily on my shoulder, dragging me down._
    - _My legs screamed with every push on the pedals._
- 内心挣扎：
    - _A voice inside urged me to stop, yet another pushed me forward._
    - _I was on the brink of giving up when…_
- 鼓励：
