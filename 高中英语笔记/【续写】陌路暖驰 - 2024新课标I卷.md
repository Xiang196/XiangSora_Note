I met Gunter on a cold, wet and unforgettable evening in September. I had planned to fly to Vienna and take a bus to Prague for a conference. Due to a big storm, my flight had been delayed by an hour and a half. I touched down in Vienna just 30 minutes before the departure of the last bus to Prague. The moment I got off the plane, I ran like crazy through the airport building and jumped into the first taxi on the rank without a second thought.

After a heated discussion that lasted for what seemed like a century, Gunter put his phone down and started the car.

Finally, with just two minutes to spare we rolled into the bus station. Thankfully, there was a long queue still waiting to board the bus. Gunter parked the taxi behind the bus, turned around, and looked at me with a big smile on his face. "We made it," he said.

Just then I realised that I had zero cash in my wallet. I flashed him an apologetic smile as I pulled out my Portuguese bankcard. He tried it several times, but the card machine just did not play along. A feeling of helplessness washed over me as I saw the bus queue thinning out.

At this moment, Gunter pointed towards the waiting hall of the bus station. There at the entrance, was a cash machine. I jumped out of the car, made a mad run for the machine, and popped my card in, only to read the message: Out of order. Sorry.

## 0.5. ✏️ **二、语言表达逐段建议**

### ✅ 第一段：

原文：

> Sweats seeped from my forehead, my heart sinking — surrounded by the unfamiliar city, in such a catch-22 dilemma, I could hardly refrain my despair at the moment when the clock told: 5 minutes left.

**建议改法（更地道 + 紧凑）**：  
→ _Sweat trickled down my forehead and my heart sank. Trapped in a catch-22 in this unfamiliar city, I could hardly hold back my despair when I glanced at the clock — only five minutes left._

- “Sweats seeped” → 用“sweat”单数 + “trickled”更自然
    
- “the clock told”略显直译，可改为“when I glanced at the clock”更生活化
    
- “refrain my despair” → 应为“refrain **from** despair”
    

---

原文：

> Gunter, however, waved his hand with a comforting smile. "Never mind, you're in hurry to attend your conference, aren't you?"

**建议小改动（语法+语气）**：  
→ _Gunter, however, waved his hand with a comforting smile. "Never mind. You're in a hurry to attend your conference, aren’t you?"_

- “in hurry” → 少了冠词，改为 “in a hurry”
    
- 这句话本身很好，体现Gunter的善良与镇定～值得保留！
    

---

原文：

> Waves of warmth surged through my heart, mixed with my undescriptable gratitude.

**建议**：  
→ “undescriptable”应为“indescribable”。建议整体优化为：  
→ _A wave of warmth surged through me, mixed with indescribable gratitude._  
更自然简练。

---

### ✅ 第二段：

原文：

> We met at the same bus station as the sun rose up high.

**建议**：  
→ _We met again at the same bus station, bathed in the golden morning sun._

- “rose up high”略显直译，可用更具画面感的表达；如加上“golden”也能传递温度感。
    

---

原文：

> The city scene around me wasn't fearful anymore, hugging me with its aesthetic atmosphere instead.

**建议**：  
→ _The city no longer felt foreign—it now embraced me with its quiet beauty._

- “wasn’t fearful”语义不太准确，“I wasn't fearful of the city”可能更合适
    
- “aesthetic atmosphere”这个词组虽不出错，但略显“翻译腔”，可以换成“quiet beauty”这种具象而温柔的表达
    

---

原文：

> "Beep—", the card machine successfully rang. "And—", I handed him the cash gratefully, "the cash, I made it, too."

**这个部分很可爱！**  
✨建议只稍微润色语调，使其更口语又有情感：  
→ _“Beep—” the card machine finally rang. “And—” I smiled, handing him the cash. “I made it, too.”_

---

原文：

> Long after the departure, I could still hear his voice — the strength lighting me up in the cold winter night and a kindness I will never forget.

**这句非常动人，保留！**  
只是建议微调句式节奏，让结尾更有余音：  
→ _Long after I left, I could still hear his voice — a quiet strength in the cold winter night, and a kindness I will never forget._
