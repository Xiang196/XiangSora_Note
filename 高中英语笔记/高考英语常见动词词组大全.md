**高考英语常见动词词组大全（含多义项及真题例句）**

**一、****Break****系列（高频考点）**

**1. break down**

- **机器****/****车辆出故障**：My car broke down on the way to the airport. (2023全国甲卷)
    
- **情绪崩溃**：He broke down when he heard the sad news.
    
- **(****谈判等****)****失败**：The negotiation broke down, so the deal failed. (2021浙江卷)
    
- **分解****(****物质****)**：Sugar can break down into glucose.
    

**2. break up**

- **(****关系****)****破裂****/****分手**：They broke up after a long relationship.
    
- **(****会议****/****聚会****)****解散**：The party broke up around midnight.
    
- **(****使****)****破碎**：The glass broke up into pieces.
    
- **分解**：The old machine was broken up for parts.
    

**3. break out**

- **(****战争****/****火灾****/****疾病****)****突然爆发**：A big fire broke out in the factory last night.
    

**4. break into**

- **强行闯入**：Thieves broke into his house and stole some money.
    
- **突然开始****(****笑****/****唱****)**：She broke into laughter at the joke.
    

**二、****Give****系列**

**1. give up**

- **放弃****(****尝试****/****希望****)**：He gave up smoking last year. (2023全国甲卷)
    
- **对****...****不抱希望**：Don't give up on your dreams.
    

**2. give in**

- **屈服****/****让步**：He refused to give in to the pressure.
    

**3. give away**

- **赠送****/****捐赠**：She gave away many books to the children in need.
    
- **泄露****(****秘密****)**：He accidentally gave away the secret.
    
- **颁发****(****奖品****)**：The principal gave away the prizes at the ceremony.
    

**4. give out**

- **分发**：Volunteers gave out free masks to the passers-by. (2024全国甲卷)
    
- **耗尽****/****用完**：His strength gave out completely after running a marathon. (2022全国丙卷)
    
- **发出****(****光****/****热****/****信号****)**：The lamp gave out a soft light.
    

**5. give back**

- **归还；送回**：Give back the book to the library on time. (2023浙江模拟卷)
    
- **回馈；回报**：He gives back to the community by volunteering.

**3. take up**

- **开始从事****(****爱好****/****职业****)**：After retirement, my father took up gardening. (2023全国Ⅱ卷)
    
- **占据****(****时间****/****空间****)**：The piano takes up too much space.
    
- **接受****(****提议****)**：She took up the challenge bravely.
    

**4. take over**

**5. take advantage of**

**6. take care of**

- **照顾；照料**：She takes care of her sick mother. (2024全国乙卷)
    
- **处理；负责**：I’ll take care of the problem.
