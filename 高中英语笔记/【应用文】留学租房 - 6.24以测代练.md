### 🧠可写内容角度梳理

#### 1. **租房原因**

- 距离学校近，方便上学（交通便利）

- 希望更自由、安静或更具隐私

#### 2. **期待建议**

- 哪些区域安全、便利、适合学生居住

## 0.2. 🪄1. 换“常见词搭配”成“地道表达”

你用的是意思没错，但表达方式太“直译”。

#### ✅ 示例升级：

|太基础的表达|地道升级表达|说明|
|---|---|---|
|**less than 500 meters far from the campus**|**within 500 meters of the campus**|“within X meters of” 是固定搭配|
|**to easily get to the campus**|**so that I can reach the campus easily** / **for convenient access to the campus**|更书面、语法更严谨|
|**feel puzzled**|**feel a bit lost / uncertain / unsure**|puzzled 用在人而不是事情上时，会有点中式感|
|**not so critical**|**not a top concern / not a big deal / not a major factor**|更自然、词汇更丰富多样|

---

## 0.3. 📐2. 用更“紧凑”的结构：合并主谓宾+从句结构

像 “The house I rented can be less than 500 meters far from the campus.”  
可以改成更流畅的**定语从句 + 名词结构**：

> I hope to find accommodation **within walking distance of the campus**.  
> = 我希望找到**步行就能到学校的住宿**

再比如：

> I hope that the house I rent can be close to the campus  
> 可改为更学术风：

> I’m looking for a place **ideally located within 500 meters of the university**.

---

## 0.4. 💼3. 用“学术表达模板”替代主观浅词

你用了 “I don’t mind if the price is a little high...”，这其实是可以换成更有逻辑感的表达：

|原句|升级句|技巧|
|---|---|---|
|I don’t mind the price|**Cost is not my main concern**|用名词替代主语句|
|the environment is important|**A quiet environment is preferable as it helps me focus**|说明原因，逻辑感强|
|the most important thing is location|**My top priority is the location**|常见书面表达“top priority”|
